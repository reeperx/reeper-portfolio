# TOGB SEO audit and content roadmap

**Date:** 29 October 2026
**Scope:** Local production build (`next start -p 3101`), homepage, `/work`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, generated metadata, sitemap, robots, JSON-LD, internal links, and a mobile Lighthouse run.
**Status:** Pre-launch. The final TOGB domain and production Vercel project are not confirmed.

## Executive summary

The technical foundation is strong: Lighthouse reports **SEO 100** and **Accessibility 100**, every inspected page has a title, description, canonical, one H1, and valid structured data, and the sitemap, robots file, manifest, security headers, and internal links are healthy.

The largest remaining gaps are not code defects:

1. **The production canonical is still the old Reeper URL.** `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` in the Vercel production environment is `https://reeper-portfolio.vercel.app`. Until that changes to the final TOGB origin, Google will be told the canonical home is the old brand.
2. **There is no real testimonial yet.** The fake carousel and three sample portraits have been removed. The testimonial section is hidden until a client approves the exact words, name, role, and any rating.
3. **There is no service-depth or case-study content.** The homepage and `/work` index are useful, but they cannot compete for broad queries such as “web design Gauteng” on their own.
4. **Search Console and Bing verification are not configured.** The code supports both verification tags, but the production environment has no tokens.
5. **Local SEO is incomplete.** There is no confirmed Google Business Profile, local citation set, or review strategy.
6. **No ranking can be guaranteed.** Google decides rankings; competitors, links, reviews, content, and proximity all matter. The work below improves the inputs and makes the results measurable.

## Findings and fixes

### 1. Production canonical and domain — high priority

**Finding:** The production environment currently sets `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://reeper-portfolio.vercel.app`. The local build falls back to `https://example.com` because `.env.local` does not set the value.

**Impact:** Canonical tags, Open Graph URLs, sitemap URLs, robots `Host`, and JSON-LD IDs all point at the wrong origin. This is the single biggest technical SEO blocker.

**Action:** Choose the final TOGB domain and Vercel project, set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to that origin in Vercel, redeploy, and confirm the canonical, sitemap, and `robots.txt` use it. Do not reuse the old Reeper URL for the TOGB launch unless that is a deliberate decision.

### 2. Real testimonials and reviews — high priority

**Finding:** The sample testimonials were removed. `testimonials` is empty, so the section is hidden and no `Review`/`AggregateRating` schema is emitted.

**Impact:** The site has less visible proof, and there is no review entity for search or local trust.

**Action:** Add only approved client words. Required: exact quote, client name, role/company, permission to publish, and whether they gave a rating. Optional: photo, source URL, and a link to the project. The code supports all of these.

**Google caveat:** Google’s review-snippet policy generally treats reviews of your own business on your own site as “self-serving” and may not display star ratings for them. The `Review` schema is still valid and useful for entity understanding, but the reliable place to earn review stars is a **Google Business Profile**. Do not invent ratings to chase stars.

### 3. Content depth — high priority

**Finding:** The site has a homepage, a work index, and two legal pages. There are no dedicated service pages, case studies, location pages, or articles.

**Impact:** Broad commercial queries such as “web design Gauteng” are dominated by agencies, directories, and established local studios. A single-page portfolio can rank for brand and some long-tail queries, but it needs more focused pages to compete for service and location searches.

**Action:**

- Publish a `/services` page with detailed scope, process, timelines, deliverables, and FAQs for launch sites, web apps, and rescues.
- Publish 2–3 case studies with the problem, constraints, decisions, stack, outcome, and a live link. Do not invent metrics; use verified facts only.
- Add internal links from the homepage and `/work` to those pages.
- Consider a Gauteng-focused page only if it has genuinely useful local content, not a thin duplicate of the homepage.

### 4. Search Console and measurement — high priority

**Finding:** `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_BING_SITE_VERIFICATION` are supported but not set in production. Vercel Web Analytics is the only measurement in place.

**Action:**

1. Verify the final domain in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
2. Add the verification tokens to Vercel and redeploy.
3. Submit `https://<final-domain>/sitemap.xml`.
4. Track branded queries, “web developer Gauteng”, “web design Gauteng”, “Next.js developer South Africa”, and the service-page queries.
5. Review Core Web Vitals monthly in Search Console after enough traffic exists.

### 5. Local SEO — high priority

**Finding:** The site now exposes a Gauteng service area, WhatsApp number, contact point, and logo. It does not have a confirmed Google Business Profile or local citation set.

**Action:**

- Create or claim the Google Business Profile as a service-area business in Gauteng.
- Use the same public name (TOGB), email, and WhatsApp number everywhere.
- Add the website and a concise service description.
- Ask real clients for Google reviews after a successful handover. Never buy or fabricate reviews.
- Add the site to relevant South African directories and local business listings where the profile is legitimate.
- Keep the site’s public copy aligned with the profile: Gauteng, South Africa; no street address unless one is genuinely public.

### 6. Performance — medium priority

**Finding:** The static testimonial change improved Performance from 51 to 73 and TBT from 3.48 s to 0.84 s. The remaining client JavaScript is mostly the interactive work grid, theme controls, contact form, and sidebar clock.

**Action:** Re-measure on the production URL. If TBT is still above 500 ms on mobile, consider server-rendering the homepage’s first four project rows and moving pagination to `/work`, which is already a complete index. Do not remove the contact form or theme controls to chase a score.

### 7. Off-page authority — ongoing

**Finding:** No backlink or digital-PR programme is in place.

**Action:** Ask satisfied clients for a link from the project credit, publish useful case studies, answer real questions in relevant communities, and pursue a small number of legitimate local links. Avoid paid link schemes and mass directories.
